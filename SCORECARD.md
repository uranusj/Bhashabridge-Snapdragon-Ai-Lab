# BhashaBridge judge scorecard

| Criterion | Evidence in package | What to show live |
|---|---|---|
| **Technical Implementation** | `PROPOSAL.md` includes the end-to-end pipeline, model strategy, NPU-first deployment, privacy controls and validation metrics. | Mixed-language transcript, local status, offline behavior, structured action items. |
| **Application Use Case & Innovation** | Solves a concrete multilingual collaboration problem and reframes translation as shared action, not just text conversion. | Three languages become one plan with owners and deadlines. |
| **Deployment & Accessibility** | Windows desktop target, offline-first design, keyboard access, captions, model profiles and clear privacy states. | Show the simple start/stop flow, high contrast output and delete-session control. |
| **Presentation & Documentation** | `PITCH.md` provides a timed story and Q&A; `README.md` is honest about prototype boundaries; sources are cited. | Open with the human problem, then make the Snapdragon advantage visible. |

## The single strongest proof point

**Airplane mode + useful result.** It demonstrates the product’s reason to exist and the platform’s role in one action.

## Claims discipline

Do not say “real-time,” “88%,” “NPU active,” “supports all Indian languages,” or “fully offline” as measured product claims until they are verified on the target HP PC. In the current concept demo, those are illustrative UI states and planned acceptance tests.
